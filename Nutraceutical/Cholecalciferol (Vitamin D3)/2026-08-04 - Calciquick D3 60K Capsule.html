Calciquick D3 60K Capsule contains Cholecalciferol 60,000 iu (International units). Cholecalciferol (Vitamin D3) is a fat soluble vitamin, that helps the body to absorb calcium and phosphorous found in food and supplements. Vitamin D is made by the body when skin is exposed to sunlight. Sunscreen, protective clothing, limited exposure to sunlight, dark skin, and age may prevent getting enough vitamin D from the sun, thus leading to Vitamin D3 Deficiency. Dr. Morepen Cholecalciferol Gelatin Capsules—most commonly marketed under the brand name Calciquick D3—are high-strength Vitamin D3 supplements used primarily to treat and prevent Vitamin D deficiency. Cholecalciferol is a fat-soluble vitamin essential for the absorption of calcium and phosphorus, playing a vital role in maintaining bone density, joint flexibility, and immune health. 
Key Product Variations
  • Calciquick D3 60K: Contains Cholecalciferol IP 60,000 IU. This is a high-dose therapeutic version typically prescribed for weekly use to correct severe deficiencies.
  • Calciquick D3 1K / 2K: Lower strength options (1,000 IU or 2,000 IU) used for daily maintenance of Vitamin D levels. 
Core Benefits
  • Enhances Calcium Absorption: Aids the body in absorbing calcium from food and other dietary supplements, ensuring it reaches the skeletal system.
  • Bone and Joint Support: Helps manage or mitigate risks associated with osteoporosis, rickets, and latent tetany.
  • Immune & Muscle Health: Promotes overall immune system regulation and reduces muscle weakness or fatigue linked to low vitamin levels. 
Usage & Dosage Guidelines
  • Frequency: The 60K (60,000 IU) formulation is highly potent and should generally be taken only once a week for 6 to 8 weeks, or as precisely directed by a healthcare professional. 
  • Administration: Swallow the capsule whole with water. Do not chew or crush it. 
  • Maximized Absorption: Take the capsule after a heavy or fat-containing meal (such as breakfast or lunch), as Vitamin D is fat-soluble and requires fats to be properly absorbed by the body. 
Safety & Side Effects
While generally well-tolerated when taken in prescribed amounts, improper usage can cause mild side effects including nausea, vomiting, headaches, constipation, or elevated calcium levels in the blood (hypercalcemia). Individuals with existing kidney stones, severe renal impairment, or high baseline calcium levels must consult a physician before using this supplement.
